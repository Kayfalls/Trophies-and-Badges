Return Policy

7-Day Return Policy

We want you to be 100% satisfied with every purchase from Trophies & Badges.


Eligible for return:

  • Item returned within 7 days of collection/delivery
  • Unused and in original condition
  • Original packaging intact
  • Proof of purchase (order reference) required

Not eligible:

  • Custom engraved or personalised items (unless defective)
  • Items returned after 7 days
  • Damaged items caused by the customer

How to return:
Email us at trophies.badges@gmail.com with your order reference and reason for return, or visit us in store at Trade Route Mall.


Refund process:
Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of receiving the returned item. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
